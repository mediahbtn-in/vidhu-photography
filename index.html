The philosophy

Life doesn't happen in poses. The best photographs remember how it felt.

From the quiet anticipation before a wedding to the first days of a new life, every story has its own rhythm. My approach is to notice the little things, preserve the honest emotions, and create photographs that feel just as meaningful years from now.

      Behind the lens

      Hello, I'm Vidhu.

      Photography, for me, is about more than creating beautiful images. It's about noticing the details, preserving the emotions, and telling the stories that make each moment unique.

      From the excitement of a wedding day to the quiet beauty of welcoming a little one, I believe every chapter deserves to be remembered in its own way.

      My goal is simple: to create photographs that bring you back to the moment, even years after it has passed.
